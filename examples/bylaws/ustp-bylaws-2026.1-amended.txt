Bylaws of the United States Transhumanist Party (example text for development and tests, not the party's bylaws)

Article I. Name.
The name of this organization is the United States Transhumanist Party.

Article II. Purpose.
The Party promotes science, technology and reason to improve the human condition.

Article III. Membership.
Membership is open to any person who supports the Party's platform.
Members in good standing may vote in Party elections.

Article IV. Officers.
The officers of the Party are the Chair, the Vice-Chair, the Secretary and the Treasurer.
Officers serve terms of three years.

Article V. Conventions.
The Party holds a convention every year.
Notice of a convention is given thirty days in advance.

Article VI. Amendments.
These bylaws may be amended by a majority of the members voting.
